# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 September 2026**

This privacy policy explains how the personal rclone Google Drive client operated by Alex Peasley handles information.

## Information accessed

When authorised through Google OAuth, the application may access information from the user's Google Drive account that is required for rclone to perform file synchronisation and file management.

This may include:

* Files and folders stored in Google Drive
* File names and metadata
* File contents when required for synchronisation
* Google account information provided as part of the OAuth authentication process

## How information is used

Information accessed through Google Drive is used solely to provide the functionality required by rclone for personal file synchronisation and management.

The information is not:

* Sold to third parties
* Used for advertising
* Used for profiling
* Shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the requested functionality

## Data storage

Google account credentials are not collected or stored by this application.

OAuth authentication is handled by Google. Any authentication tokens required by rclone are stored and managed by the rclone installation on the user's device.

## Data sharing

Google Drive data is not intentionally shared with third parties.

The application is intended for personal use and does not operate as a public service.

## Data retention and deletion

Because this application is used for personal file synchronisation, data accessed from Google Drive may remain in the user's Google Drive account and on devices where rclone is configured.

Removing the rclone configuration or revoking the application's access through the user's Google Account will prevent further authenticated access.

## Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect OAuth credentials and authentication tokens. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the application or its data handling practices change.
